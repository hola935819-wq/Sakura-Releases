## ⚡ Quick Start

1. Download **[`SakuraInjector.exe`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hola935819-wq/Sakura-Releases/main/SakuraInjector.exe)**.
2. Launch Minecraft (1.21.11 Fabric Loader or Vanilla).
3. Open `SakuraInjector.exe` and click anywhere to detect your game.
4. Select your Minecraft process and click **Inject Sakura Client**.
5. Press `RIGHT SHIFT` or `INSERT` in-game to toggle the ClickGUI!
